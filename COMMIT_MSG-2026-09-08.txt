Carrier reminders: one decision tree, a manual push button in CC, and a nightly job

Every active carrier now resolves to exactly one reminder, decided server-side in
app_private.reminder_for_carrier(). The checks run hardest-blocker-first, so the
rule that matters is structural rather than incidental: a carrier who added a
DRIVER first and still has no truck gets the truck email and can never receive
the driver email.

  onboarding not finished  -> nothing (cc_run_onboarding_reminders owns them)
  no truck                 -> truck_continue (draft open) / truck_add
  trucks but none usable   -> truck_continue   (no VIN / no payload)
  usable truck, no driver  -> driver_add
  fleet ready              -> avail_confirm / avail_continue / avail_start
  confirmed in last 24h    -> nothing

Verified on staging with a 14-case truth table before production. Two real
defects were found by that test and are fixed here:

* The fleet-ready branch ran BEFORE the incomplete-truck branch, so a carrier
  whose only truck had no VIN and no payload was told to go post availability —
  a truck that cannot be dispatched. Reordered.

* The audience joined organization_memberships without collapsing to one person
  per carrier, so a carrier with two members appeared twice and the actual
  recipient was decided by join order. A driver account could end up being the
  one person told to go fix the fleet. The idempotency key hid it: one email
  went out, just not reliably to the right person. Now exactly one recipient per
  carrier, preferring the account owner.

Measured on production first (46 active carriers): 30 are 'not_started' in
document onboarding with no verified COI. trg_fleet_truck_coi_gate refuses to
insert a truck without a verified COI (LB004), so "add your first truck" would
have sent those 30 to a form that turns them away — while the 14:00 UTC
onboarding cron is already emailing them about documents. They are now silent
here. Real target set: 10 carriers.

Sender identity is pinned in meta.category rather than inferred: the keys are
carrier.reminder.*, and delivery-worker's DISPATCH_RE matches both "carrier" and
"driver", which is the same trap its own v10 note describes.

Guard rails on both the button and the cron, because they call one function:
marketing opt-out, suppression list, cadence (availability once a day; onboarding
stops after 4 nudges, 3 days apart), one queue per carrier per reminder per day,
and a missing template SKIPS the carrier instead of sending something wrong.

The nightly job lb-carrier-reminders (15:00 UTC, an hour after the onboarding
cron) is registered on both databases and left DISABLED. Nothing reaches a real
carrier until the six emails have been reviewed and it is switched on:
  select cron.alter_job(
    (select jobid from cron.job where jobname='lb-carrier-reminders'), active := true);
