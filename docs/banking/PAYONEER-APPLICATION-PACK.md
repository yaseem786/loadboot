## ⛔ STEP 0 — Pehle purana account resolve karein (yeh sab se ahem hai)

Aap ne ~3 saal pehle apne naam par Payoneer banaya tha aur ab login yaad nahin.

**Naya individual account banane ki koshish HARGIZ na karein.** Payoneer ek shakhs ka ek hi account
allow karta hai. Wohi passport/ID dobara dene par system **duplicate account** flag karta hai — natija
reject, ya dono accounts freeze.

**Karna kya hai:**
1. payoneer.com → Login → **"Forgot your password?"** — apni purani emails ek ek kar ke try karein
   (gmail, koi purana business email).
2. Na chale to **Payoneer support** se raabta karein (site par Contact Us / live chat). Unhein batayein:
   *"I opened a Payoneer account around 2023 but I no longer have access. Please help me identify
   whether it is still active."* Naam + passport number + purani email guesses dein — wo record se
   dhoond lete hain.
3. Support jo bhi kahe, uska **screenshot/email mehfooz rakhein**:
   - Account **band ho chuka hai** → to saaf raasta, naya bana sakte hain.
   - Account **active hai** → us tak dobara access lein. Yeh actually **acha** hai (neeche dekhein).

⏱️ Yeh 1–3 din le sakta hai. Company account isi ke baad.

## ⚠️ STEP 0.5 — Withdrawal ka masla, aur uska hal

Payoneer ka apna qaida: **company account ka withdrawal bank company ke naam par hona chahiye.**
LoadBoot LLC ka to kahin koi bank account hai hi nahin — yehi to poora masla hai.

**Hal (isi liye purana individual account qeemti hai):**

```
US carriers  ──ACH/card──►  Payoneer COMPANY account (LoadBoot LLC)
                                      │
                    ┌─────────────────┼──────────────────┐
                    │                 │                  │
            Payoneer Mastercard   "Make a Payment"   (baad mein)
            (kharche/ATM)          → apna INDIVIDUAL   US bank account
                                     Payoneer account
                                          │
                                     HBL/Meezan (aap ke naam par) ✅
```

Har qadam par naam match karta hai: company→company, individual→individual. Yeh owner draw hai.

**➡️ Apply karne se PEHLE Payoneer support se yeh sawal likhit poochein:**
> "I am opening a company account for my US LLC. The LLC does not yet have a bank account anywhere.
> Can I complete registration without linking a company bank account, and later withdraw by paying my
> own individual Payoneer account as an owner draw?"

Unka jawab hi tay karega ke aage kaise barhna hai. Chat par jawab jaldi mil jata hai. **Andaza laga kar
form mat bharein** — ghalat jawab se account review mein atak jata hai.

## 🚫 Reject hone ki aam wajuhat — in se bachein

1. **VPN ya anti-detect browser se apply karna.** Payoneer ke fraud systems IP/device ko declared address
   se match karte hain. Apne aam Pakistani connection aur aam Chrome se apply karein. Yeh sab se aam
   avoidable rejection hai.
2. **Duplicate account** — Step 0 isi liye pehle hai.
3. **Naam ka farq** — passport, Articles, EIN letter, aur form: chaaron par naam bilkul aik jaisa ho.
   "LoadBoot LLC" vs "Loadboot L.L.C." bhi masla ban sakta hai.
4. **Gmail address** company account par.
5. **Business description mein "payments/marketplace/escrow"** ka lafz.
6. **Barhaya hua volume.**
7. **Reply mein deri** — agar wo mazeed document maangein to 24 ghante mein jawab dein.
