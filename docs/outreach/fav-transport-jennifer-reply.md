Hi Jennifer,

Thanks for reaching out, and congratulations on getting FAV Transport on the road. Answering your
questions in the order you asked them.

**Hotshot and new authorities.** Both are core for us, not an exception we make. Hotshot — class 3–5
with goosenecks and deck-overs — is freight we move every week, and new-authority carriers are a
large share of the carriers we onboard.

One honest caveat so nothing surprises you: a share of brokers will not set up an MC under 90 days
or under six months, no matter who calls them. That is a broker credit rule, not a dispatcher
problem, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling. What we do is work the brokers who *do* set
up new authorities, keep your record clean from day one, and build you a repeat broker list so that
when you clear the age gates you already have relationships instead of starting over. Your first
60 days are about earning reloads from the handful of brokers who said yes — not spraying the board.

**Lanes.** North Carolina and the Southeast is exactly where hotshot pays, so that stays your base.
We only put you on a longer run when the all-in rate and a realistic backhaul out of that market
justify it — and you see all of that before anything is booked.

**Fee.** A flat **5% of gross** on loads we book. Nothing upfront, no setup fee, no monthly
subscription, and nothing charged in a week we don't book you. On a $2,400 load that is $120.

**Contract.** None. No term, no notice period, cancel whenever you want. If we are not earning the
5%, you should be free to walk that day.

**No forced dispatch.** Every load reaches you with the rate, loaded miles, deadhead, stops and
appointment times before we accept it. You say yes or no. We never book over you.

**What the 5% covers:** load search and booking, rate negotiation, all broker communication, carrier
setup packets with each new broker, rate confirmation and paperwork handling, check calls and
appointment scheduling, factoring/NOA coordination, and forward lane planning so you are not sitting
empty waiting on the next call.

One point about how we operate, because it protects you: we work **for the carrier** and book under
FAV Transport's own authority. We never touch the freight money — brokers pay you, or your factoring
company, directly. Any dispatcher who wants payments routed through them is a red flag worth
walking away from.

**To get you set up we would need:**

- MC and DOT numbers
- Certificate of insurance — the PDF from your agent, showing auto liability and cargo limits
- Your FMCSA operating authority letter
- A signed W-9
- Voided check or bank letter — or your factoring company's NOA if you factor
- The signed dispatch agreement (we send it, e-sign takes about two minutes)
- Driver name(s) and CDL, plus truck and trailer details: year, make, deck length, capacity

With those in hand you can be taking loads the same day.

Easiest next step is a 10-minute call so I can hear what you are pulling and what you want to make

works for you today or tomorrow? Or reply here and I will send the agreement and the secure upload
