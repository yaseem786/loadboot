The pattern is always the same: an unverified MC, a rate far off the lane average, re-tendered freight — and the truck that shows up isn't the carrier you booked. Between 2022–2025 that pattern cost brokers roughly $4 billion.

The fix isn't exciting, but it works: verify authority and insurance at booking (not after), exchange carrier packets automatically, and replace check calls with GPS tracking and geofenced arrive/depart stamps — one shared record both sides can't argue with.
