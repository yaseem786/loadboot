# LoadBoot Command Center — Cut Audit
**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Scope:** all 86 files in `app/command-center/views/` + `app.js` router + `views/shell.js` NAV
**Sources of truth:** `cc_inventory.json` (LOC/RPCs/inline-styles/nav), `cc_data_counts.json` (prod row counts 2026-09-02 + feature flags), read of every view file.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

You have **86 view files, 75 routes and 73 nav items** for a business with **1 staff member, 6 trucks, 15 loads, 1 trip and 47 carrier orgs**. Of the 86 files, **20 are backed by database tables with literally zero rows** after months in production — campaigns, audiences, announcements, team chat, workflows, webhook endpoints, trip exceptions, trip incidents, emergency requests, booking requests, referral commissions, report definitions, SEO keywords, redirects, API keys, comm threads, partner records, account violations, dispatcher bookings, load offers. Another **13 are second or third views over data another screen already owns** (three loads boards, four analytics screens, three FMCSA surfaces, three notification surfaces, two campaign managers, two automation admins, two directories). The parts that are actually used — live chat (62 conversations), automation tasks (157), documents (72), agents (65), carriers (47), leads (35), dispatchers (31), templates (49) — are also the parts most worth keeping. **Recommendation: 35 KEEP, 27 MERGE, 14 HIDE, 6 DELETE, 4 REDESIGN → a 24-item nav in 6 groups.**

Three views (`dispatchers.js`, `outreach.js`, `out/liveChat.js`) are genuinely big-brand standard. **51 of 86 files score 3/5 or below against them.** Average design score: **3.21 / 5**.
