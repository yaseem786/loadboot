What changes, and why. (1) The triage strip replaces the chip row. Today the seven counts are inline pills and the detail underneath is a single joined string — Awaiting RC: Hinesville → Savannah (Abdul Rafeh, 41 min) · New Richmond → Indianapolis (Sana K., 2 h) — which cannot be scanned, sorted or clicked per item. Each count becomes a filter that drives the list below it. (2) An RC row becomes a row. Lane, money and $/mi, pickup countdown, and the risk flags each get their own column, so “below floor” and “pickup in 4 h” are seen before the Approve button is reached — instead of being three of six pills competing with the lane text. (3) Money is a number, not a pill. “to pay” becomes $508, because the question a supervisor is actually asking is how much is owed, not how many rows exist. (4) The roster answers the supervisor’s real questions in columns — who has carriers, who posted availability this morning, how fast their RCs clear, who has unread carrier messages, what they are owed — rather than a status pill and a name.
Four things the dispatcher portal knows and the Command Center does not show. The portal has nine tabs — Today, Board, Trucks, Bookings, Brokers, Money, Messages, Packet, My KPIs — and the supervisor view surfaces almost none of them. (1) Availability freshness. The portal asks every dispatcher for a daily availability line before 06:00 ET. Nothing in the Command Center shows who has not posted it — yet that is the first thing that goes wrong, and it silently costs a day of matching. It is a column above. (2) Check-call lag. The portal logs check calls; the CC shows a “last touch” only for moving loads, buried in a joined line. Stale-touch belongs in the triage strip as its own count. (3) Broker setup / packet completeness. The portal has Brokers and Packet tabs — the four broker-setup documents. The CC has no view of which dispatcher has how many broker setups done, which is the constraint on how much freight they can actually source. (4) Their own KPIs. The dispatcher sees “My KPIs”; the supervisor cannot see the same numbers side by side across the team, which is what a weekly review needs.
