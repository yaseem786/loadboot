The time a driver loses at your dock, they try to make up on the road.

There is a version of this that is not adversarial. Detention becomes a fight when the terms were never written down — so the fix is to write them down:

• Detention terms on the rate confirmation before the load is booked, not negotiated after
• Arrival measured by GPS at the gate, so nobody is arguing about hand-written times
• The notice sent before free time expires, timestamped, on the record
• The claim assembled from that trip data and settled on the same invoice as the freight

Symmetric evidence is the point. When the carrier and the broker are both looking at the same arrival record, detention stops being a negotiation and becomes a line item — and facilities that habitually hold trucks show up in your own data, so you can fix the dock instead of fighting the driver.
