## 6. Posting rules that make it "viral" (platform mechanics, not magic)
- Link goes in the FIRST COMMENT, never in the body (FB and LinkedIn both suppress external links). Company-page post is the exception — link is fine there.
- Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes; ask a question back.
- Best windows (US Central): Tue–Thu 7–9 am & 5–7 pm (drivers on break), Sunday 6–9 pm (planning the week).
- Post the personal LinkedIn version FIRST, then reshare from the company page 24 h later with one added line.
- Share the FB post into 3–5 owner-operator groups (e.g. hot shot / dry van / new-authority groups) as a plain-text version + image — group rules permitting.
- Turn post 2B ("Math post") into a Story/Reel with `story-1080x1920.png`.

## Do NOT claim (no data exists yet)
- Number of carriers/loads/brokers on the platform, "rated #1", "trusted by X drivers", any testimonial or dollar-saved average. Only the fee (5%), the model and the flat "no contract / no monthly fee" facts are verifiable.
- Never write "we arrange your freight" in shipper copy (anti-brokering rule) — shipper copy says shippers post and pay carriers direct.
