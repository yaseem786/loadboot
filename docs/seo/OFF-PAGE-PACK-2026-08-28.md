## ⚠️ Read this before spending a weekend on links

Two honest cautions, both from this project's own data:

1. **Links are not currently the binding constraint.** 28 pages are built and undeployed, and
   `market-rates.html` alone is 44% of all site impressions at ~0 CTR because it cannibalises
   ~140 rate queries. Deploying fixes more than any five directory submissions will.
2. **Do NOT launch on Product Hunt yet.** It is the single biggest one-shot in the playbook —
   and it sends a few thousand people to a load board that currently shows a real carrier
   **zero loads**. You get one launch. Spend it after there is freight on the board, not before.

## Priority order — what to do with the next three hours

### 1. Connectively / Qwoted / Featured.com — the highest-value recurring 15 minutes (do first)
Press-quote platforms. A single answered query can produce a link from a publication with
far more authority than any directory. It is 15 minutes a week, forever.

Sign up at all three, beat: **logistics, transportation, trucking, small business, supply chain**.
Then answer one query per week. Template:

> Hi <name> — I run LoadBoot, a US trucking load board and dispatch platform.
> On "<their topic>": <two or three sentences with a real, specific number you can stand
> behind — e.g. the FMCSA cargo-securement tiedown rule, current DOE diesel at $5.65/gal
> national average for the week of 24 August, or the $50–$100/hr detention range with two
> free hours>. Happy to expand on any of it, or point you at the primary source.
> — Muhammad Yaseen, founder, LoadBoot (loadboot.com)

**Use only figures you can source.** Your credibility is the product here. Diesel comes from
the EIA weekly survey; securement rules from 49 CFR 393; detention/TONU/layover ranges from
your own published accessorial standards.

### 2. G2 and Capterra vendor listings (~45 min each, one-time)
These are where brokers and carriers actually compare software, so they bring qualified
traffic as well as a link.
- **G2** → g2.com → "Add your product" → category *Transportation Management Systems* or
  *Freight Management*. Free listing. They will ask for a logo (use `logo-full.png`) and
  screenshots (the board, the tracking map, a rate confirmation).
- **Capterra** → vendor signup → free listing. **GetApp and Software Advice are the same
  network** and pick up the listing automatically, so one submission is three profiles.
- Note: the Capterra *badge* was deliberately removed from the site earlier. That was about
  displaying an unearned badge — it has nothing to do with having the listing itself.

### 3. Foundation profiles (~30 min total, one-time)
Google Business Profile (Trucking/Logistics service) · Bing Places · LinkedIn Company page ·
X · Facebook Page. Mostly nofollow — the value is brand consistency and showing up when
someone searches your name, not link equity. Same tagline and description everywhere.

### 4. Resource-page outreach — 5 emails, from hello@loadboot.com (~40 min)
Your genuinely linkable assets are the **free no-signup calculators** (`tools.html`,
`cost-per-mile-calculator.html`) and the **market-rates table**. Target small and mid-size
trucking blogs with a "resources" or "tools" page — not big news sites.

> **Subject:** Free trucking calculator for your resources page
>
> Hi — I saw your <name the exact page, in your own words, first line>.
> We built a free trucking calculator suite — cost per mile, profit per load, detention —
> with no signup and no email wall: https://loadboot.com/tools.html
> If it is useful for your readers, feel free to add it. No strings, it stays free.
> — Yaseen, LoadBoot

> **Subject:** Weekly truckload rate table you can cite
>
> Hi — your <their article> quotes rates from <year/source>.
> We publish national truckload benchmarks by equipment — dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck
> and four more — with what the carrier is paid, what brokers buy and sell at, and what
> shippers pay: https://loadboot.com/market-rates.html
> Cite it or ignore it, either is fine. Happy to answer questions on the methodology.
> — Yaseen, LoadBoot

**The first line has to name their actual page in your own words**, or it reads as a mail
merge and gets deleted. Five personalised emails beat fifty templated ones.

### 5. Community answers — value first, link only when it fits (ongoing)
r/Truckers, r/FreightBrokers, TruckersReport, Quora. Build account age first; a new account
dropping links is removed as spam. Format: three or four lines answering the question from
your own knowledge, then "full breakdown here: <link>" **only if it genuinely adds more**.

| Question you will see | Page to point at |
|---|---|
| "how do I claim detention pay?" | `detention-pay-policy.html` |
| "are load boards full of fake loads?" | `ghost-loads-load-board-problems.html` |
| "what is a rate con / TONU?" | `how-to-read-a-rate-confirmation.html`, `tonu-policy.html` |
| "how much do dispatchers charge?" | `how-much-does-a-truck-dispatcher-cost.html` |
| "what is a lumper fee?" | `lumper-policy.html` |
| "how is fuel surcharge calculated?" | `fuel-surcharge-trucking.html` |

### 6. Deferred on purpose
- **Product Hunt** — after the board has real loads. See the caution above.
- **Paid link packages, Fiverr, PBNs** — never. Rule #1 of the playbook, and it is correct.
