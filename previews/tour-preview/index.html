Kya dekhna hai

Yeh asli carrier portal ki CSS aur shell hai, data nakli hai. Tour engine wahi hai jo product mein jayega.

  • Role badlein: owner 11 stops, driver 6, dispatcher 8. Har ek ka apna lehja.
  • Phone par card neeche sheet ban jata hai, tab bar ke upar. Target neeche ho to upar chala jata hai.
  • Tour khud screen badalta hai: Load Board, My Loads, Documents, Finance.
  • Neeche daayen ? button: replay, screen guide, quick tips, support.
  • Tour ke baad naye screen par ek baar nudge aata hai: "New here?"
  • Empty data: naya account, kuch booked nahi. Setup card aur "three taps" wale stops khud hat jate hain, load board aur My Loads apna khali-halat text dikhate hain.
  • Desktop par ← → Esc chalte hain. Spotlight ke bahar click block hai.
