The LoadBoot standard

Four rules every posting on this board obeys

Most boards let anyone post anything. Ours costs money to abuse — so what you see is real, priced, and yours to approve.

Rate card first

Detention, TONU and layover terms are printed on every posting — you read them before you accept, not after the argument.

How it's enforcedA posting without a full rate card cannot go live.

Zero ghost loads

Stale postings auto-close. Late cancellations carry TONU exposure for the poster. Fakes cost money here, so they don't scale.

How it's enforcedBrokers are screened live on FMCSA; new brokerages start with a small posting allowance.

Real deadhead

Empty miles are measured from where your truck actually sits — your last drop or posted location — not from the nearest city center.

How it's enforcedOffers show loaded miles, deadhead and RPM already worked out.

You approve

Nothing books without your OK. Before the driver moves, LoadBoot checks the rate confirmation against your floor rate.

How it's enforcedAccept, counter or decline in one tap — your call, every load.

Available sources depend on your authority, equipment and eligibility. LoadBoot is an independent dispatch service and marketplace — not a freight broker; when broker authority is legally required, freight moves through licensed broker partners.
