What changed vs. the live header

  • 1
    Mega panel, not a 264px dropdown. Panel spans the content width and is grouped by task (Book & move / Get paid / More).
  • 2
    Icons + descriptions. Each key page gets a 38px icon tile that turns brand-blue on hover.
  • 3
    Per-menu promo card. Navy card with the route-line motif from the portal auth panel; Solutions shows the three account portals.
  • 4
    Right-side drawer on mobile. Accordions, 44px rows, CTAs pinned at the bottom.

Design notes

Everything is derived from the owner-locked brand kit — no new colors introduced.

Palette

Navy ink, blue for interaction, orange reserved for the single primary CTA and the topbar pulse dot. Neutrals are blue-biased (#F4F7FB / #E2E8F1), not flat grey.

Type

Manrope 700/800 for nav labels, row titles and promo headings; Inter 400 for descriptions. Eyebrows are 0.66rem, uppercase, 0.12em tracking.

Motion

Panel fades and rises 8px on a 220ms ease-out; caret rotates and turns blue; icon tile lifts 1px. Honors prefers-reduced-motion.
