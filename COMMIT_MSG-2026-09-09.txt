Carrier reminders: cover the whole onboarding funnel, and put the button where the work happens

Yesterday's engine only knew the second half of the funnel — truck, driver,
availability. The document half and email confirmation were owned by a different
system with no screen, no preview and no manual button. Measured on production
(47 active carriers), that meant the new screen could see 11 of them and was blind
to the other 36.

One decision tree now answers for every stage, hardest blocker first:

  email never confirmed   -> confirm_email     (they cannot even sign in)
  a document rejected     -> docs_fix          (named, because they think it is done)
  no documents at all     -> docs_start
  documents partly done   -> docs_finish       ("2 of 4 verified", names the rest)
  no truck                -> truck_add / truck_continue
  usable truck, no driver -> driver_add
  fleet ready             -> avail_start / avail_continue / avail_confirm

It stays SILENT in two states on purpose: documents sitting in our review queue,
and a fully verified carrier waiting on an activation decision from us. The ball is
on our side there. Chasing somebody for something already on our desk is how you
teach people to ignore your email.

The document emails name the actual documents. delivery-worker does no merge-field
substitution by design, so {{MISSING_LIST}} and {{DOC_PROGRESS}} are filled at queue
time from carrier_onboarding_state, the plain-text half is rebuilt from the
substituted HTML so the two can never disagree, and any unfilled token is stripped
before queueing — a carrier must never receive a literal {{...}}.

Carrier 360 had a "Send first-truck reminder" button and nothing else, so a carrier
with a truck and no driver had no button at all. Replaced with one "Next reminder"
card that asks the same engine what THIS carrier is blocked on and sends the matching
email. It shows the subject that will go out, the documents by name, when it last
went, how many times, and whether the carrier is reachable at all (no email / opted
out / suppressed are three different answers). When the cadence says not due, the
button becomes "Send anyway" behind a confirm — an override only a person gets; the
nightly job can never take it.

Two systems could have emailed the same carrier on the same day, so the old cron's
two carrier nags are retired. It keeps the "your documents are with our compliance
team" reassurance, the staff activation alert, the in-app bell, and the entire agent
loop. That edit rewrites the function from its own source rather than restating 200
lines of it.

Things this caught along the way:

* 'if not v_confirmed then' occurs TWICE in cc_run_onboarding_reminders — once in the
  carrier loop, once in the agent loop. The obvious guard would have silently killed
  agent activation emails. The migration counts its anchors and aborts rather than guess.

* Adding p_org to reminder_dispatch created an OVERLOAD, not a replacement. The old
  4-argument version survived with the old body and every existing caller kept
  resolving to it as the exact match. Dropped explicitly.

* Carrier reminders was a tab under Insights & Admin > Templates, where nobody looking
  after carriers would ever find it. It is a daily carrier-ops screen, so it now has
  its own nav item under Carriers.

Also promoted bl_mail_0335 (CC Mailbox hardening) to production. It had been applied
to staging only, so the merged Mailbox UI was calling cc_mail_stats and a 4-argument
cc_mail_list that did not exist here — which is exactly the PGRST202 the screen showed.
Verified function-for-function against staging after applying.

The nightly job (lb-carrier-reminders, 15:00 UTC) stays DISABLED on both databases.
Nothing reaches a real carrier until the ten emails have been reviewed:
  select cron.alter_job(
    (select jobid from cron.job where jobname='lb-carrier-reminders'), active := true);
