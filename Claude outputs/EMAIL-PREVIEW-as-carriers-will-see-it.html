Dispatch update

Your dispatcher can only sell a truck they can see.

When we don’t know where your truck is this morning — or where it delivers and when it frees up — we are guessing. Guessing loses loads to the carrier who told their dispatcher first.

So starting today, your dedicated LoadBoot dispatcher works your loads only while your truck’s availability is posted and confirmed in the app within the last 24 hours. No fresh post, no sourcing. Same rule the best fleets already run internally.

30 seconds · every morning
1Open the app → Load Board → Post your availability.
2Empty? Pick the state, then the city, and how far you’ll drive to pick up — add the ZIP for exact deadhead miles.
Booked? Post where it delivers, the delivery date, and where you want the backhaul to go. We start on the reload before you unload.
3Tomorrow, tap “✓ Still available today” — or update it if the truck moved. Every post expires automatically 24h after you last confirmed it; an expired one shows a Repost button.
Post my truck now →

Needs at least one truck and one driver in your Fleet tab — the app takes you there if they’re missing.

Why the app, not WhatsApp or email

By the time a message is read, the load is gone. A posted truck is matched against every load on our board the second it goes live, alerts you automatically, and puts your truck in front of your dispatcher and every shipper and broker posting into that lane.

Your Dashboard now shows exactly one of three states, so there’s never a doubt:

Dispatcher working your loads — confirmed today
Confirm today — your post is over 24 hours old
Dispatcher paused — nothing posted

Post it this morning and your dispatcher starts on it today.

While you’re in there

Your books are in the same app

Most carriers don’t know this is sitting in their account, so here it is plainly. Under Finance you already have:

🟢QuickBooks Online — connect once and every delivered-load invoice and expense pushes across on its own, and payments pull back. No double entry.
⛽Expenses — fuel, tolls, maintenance, lumper in about 30 seconds at the pump. It’s also what makes your cost-per-mile real instead of a guess.
🗺IFTA — enter state miles as you run them and the quarter is already done when the filing is due, instead of a weekend of receipts.
👥Payroll — what you owe your drivers and what’s already paid, without a spreadsheet.

Costs nothing extra and only you can edit it. Our staff can view it read-only if you ask for help — nobody can change your books.
