### DM bhejne se pehle 3-GATE (kabhi skip nahi)
1. **Broker authority** — `POST https://rwscphuhpjoudvljvmdk.supabase.co/functions/v1/fmcsa-verify`
   body `{"mc": <number>}`. Broker-only entity par top-level `authority` "inactive" dikhta hai —
   asal jawab `liDockets[].broker = "A"` hai.
2. **Credit** — 123LB par days-to-pay 35+ ya grade D = pass.
3. **Factoring approval** — booking se pehle Truckstop `FactoringAR@truckstop.com` se broker credit approval.

### FB-specific red flags (ye alag hain, aur zyada khatarnak)
- Load post par **koi MC number nahi** → 90% fake ya double-brokered.
- "Send your MC and COI first, I'll send the rate con" **bina company naam ke** → identity harvesting.
- Rate con jo kisi aur MC ke naam aaye → double brokering, foran walk away.
- Gmail/Yahoo se broker email + company domain nahi → verify hone tak kuch mat bhejo.
- Payment "after delivery, no factoring allowed" → chargeback ka risk.

## Sahi rasta: carrier ke account par **dispatcher seat** — aur ye AUP-legal hai

Ye wo farq hai jo sab se ahem hai, aur jise log ghalat samajhte hain:

| Cheez | Halal ya haram |
|---|---|
| Carrier ka username/password use karna | ❌ **AUP violation.** Kabhi nahi. |
| Carrier ke account par apna **alag user + apna password** banwana | ✅ **DAT ka apna banaya hua tareeqa hai.** |

Dispatcher seat mein aap ko **apna login milta hai** — apna email, apna password, apna audit trail.
Carrier ka login chhuna hi nahi parta. Ye bilkul wohi cheez hai jo DAT khud recommend karta hai.

### Justin (Munster) ke account par seat lene ka poora tareeqa

**Justin ko ye karna hai (aap nahi kar sakte — account owner hi kar sakta hai):**

1. `account.dat.com` par apne DAT credentials se login
2. **Subscriptions** → **+ ADD SEATS**
3. Plus sign se seat quantity 1 barhaye → pricing dekhe → **Next**
4. **Service Agreement Quote** aayega (purana monthly cost vs naya) → neeche **teenon confirmation boxes** tick → **ACCEPT AND SUBMIT**
5. Blue banner ka intezar — seat ready hone mein **20 minute tak** lag sakte hain
6. **User Management** → upar dayen **Create a New User**
7. Dispatcher ki profile info bhare (naam: LoadBoot Dispatch, email: `dispatch@loadboot.com`)
8. **Seat Assignments** box tick kare → **Create User**

**Phir hamari taraf:**

9. `dispatch@loadboot.com` par DAT ki email aayegi → **SET MY PASSWORD**
10. Apna password banayein (⚠️ **user khud banaye aur khud enter kare** — password rule)
