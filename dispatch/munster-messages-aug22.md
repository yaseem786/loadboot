## 2. Brokers — email/WhatsApp ka asal jawab

**WhatsApp: nahi.** In paanch mein se kisi ne bhi SMS ya WhatsApp number publish nahi kiya, aur ye poori US brokerage industry ka norm hai. Wajah practical hai — carrier setup mein W-9, insurance certificate, aur signed broker–carrier agreement chahiye hota hai, aur broker ye sab ek **audit-able portal** mein chahta hai, chat thread mein nahi (double-brokering fraud ki wajah se ye aur sakht hua hai). WhatsApp aap ko **milega** — lekin ulta taraf se, load tracking ping ke tor par.

**To rasta ye hai: portal mein set up ho jao → phone par rate negotiate karo → email par rate con aur documents.**
