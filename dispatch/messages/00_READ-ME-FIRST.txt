MUNSTER DISPATCH - MESSAGE FILES
======================================================================

Why these exist: a spreadsheet cell that holds several lines does not
copy cleanly - Excel and Google Sheets wrap it in quote marks, or paste
it as a table. These plain text files do not have that problem.

HOW TO USE
  1. Double-click the file you need. It opens in Notepad.
  2. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste into your email or the 123Loadboard message box.
  4. Replace anything in [SQUARE BRACKETS].

Files ending 1-MESSAGE are the board message or the phone script.
Files ending 2-EMAIL carry the TO address and SUBJECT at the top.

Nothing here has been sent to any broker. You send all of it.
