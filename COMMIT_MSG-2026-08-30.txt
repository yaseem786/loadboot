3. DISPATCHER PROGRAMME (app/agent/, app/command-center/)
================================================================
app/agent/dispatcher-workspace.js - the ET-first workspace: clock strip,
  server-timestamped Today queue, board, trucks + availability, log and
  edit bookings with RC attach, message thread, commission/payout
  columns, packet, KPI bar.
Deep links throughout the workspace - #bookings/<id>, #bookings/new,
  #trucks/<id>/availability, #messages/<assignmentId>,
  #board/<assignmentId>, #brokers/new, plus Today-queue rows that open
  their own subject.
Realtime - broadcast + presence on channel dispatch:live, wired into
  both the workspace and the CC dispatchers view. Every event is a
  refetch HINT, never data; the 90-second fallback poll stays.
CC dispatchers premium view - command bar with a live ET clock and
  presence avatars, a 7-cell triage strip that filters (RC to approve /
  awaiting RC / moving / stale > 4h / unread / to pay out / trials <= 3d),
  ticking pickup and waiting timers, risk-flag column, Approve disabled
  until an RC file exists, live-wire rail, owed-$ pill per dispatcher.
  No new backend - stale is derived from last_touch_min, money from
  cc_dispatcher_commission_list.
app/carrier/dispatcher-card.js - the carrier's "meet your dispatcher"
  card, the ?ack= acknowledgement flow, dispatch@ + WhatsApp contact
  (never the dispatcher's personal number).
