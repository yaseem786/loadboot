# Master Shipper–Carrier Transportation Terms — v1 (DRAFT)

>
> This is a **template the shipper and the carrier sign between themselves**:
> - the shipper signs it once in the LoadBoot portal;
> - a carrier accepts it for each load when it accepts that load's Rate Agreement.
>
> **LoadBoot is not a party.** It only supplies the software that shows, signs and stores the document
> (FMCSA 2023 guidance: being a "named party on the shipping contract" points to broker status — so
> LoadBoot must never be one).
>
> Text in **[square brackets]** is a blank. An attorney review is still recommended, especially for
> Section 10 (liability) and Section 16 (law/disputes).

## 1. Parties and how these Terms apply

1.1 These Terms are between:
- the **Shipper**, who signed them in the LoadBoot portal; and
- each **Carrier**, an FMCSA-authorized motor carrier that accepts a Rate Agreement for one of the Shipper's loads.

1.2 A contract for a load is formed when **the Shipper accepts the Carrier** for that load **and the Carrier accepts the Rate Agreement**. The Rate Agreement is generated in the LoadBoot portal from the Shipper's posting.

1.3 Order of precedence:
1. the Rate Agreement;
2. these Terms;
3. the bill of lading.

The bill of lading serves as the receipt for the goods. Carrier tariffs, rules circulars and printed bill-of-lading terms do not apply unless the Rate Agreement says so.

1.4 **LoadBoot LLC is not a party** to these Terms, to any Rate Agreement or to any shipment. LoadBoot:
- does not arrange the transportation;
- does not set the rate;
- does not select the Carrier;
- does not handle freight charges;
- has no liability under these Terms.

## 2. Carrier's status and promises

The Carrier promises, for every load, that:
- (a) it is a motor carrier registered with FMCSA, with active operating authority for the transportation;
- (b) it does not hold an "Unsatisfactory" safety rating;
- (c) it carries at least:
  - the automobile liability insurance required by 49 CFR Part 387, and never less than the amount stated in the Rate Agreement;
  - cargo insurance of at least the amount stated in the Rate Agreement;
- (d) it will tell the Shipper at once if any of the above changes before delivery;
- (e) its drivers are qualified, licensed and, for hazardous materials, properly endorsed and trained.

## 3. No re-brokering

3.1 The Carrier will move the load **with its own equipment and its own drivers**, or drivers under its exclusive control.

3.2 The Carrier will **not** broker, re-broker, co-broker, assign, subcontract or interline the load without the Shipper's prior written consent.

3.3 A breach of this section is a material breach. The Carrier is then liable for all loss that results, and no freight charge is owed for that load until the matter is resolved.

## 4. Rates and accessorials

4.1 The rate in the Rate Agreement is **all-in** except for accessorials that are **listed in it**.

4.2 **Detention.** Detention is paid under the free time and hourly rate for that location, as stated in the Rate Agreement. It is measured from the Carrier's on-time arrival for the appointment. The Carrier must support it with an arrival and departure record, such as a portal GPS/geofence stamp or a signed in/out time.

4.3 **Lumper and loading.** Loading and unloading follow the location's stated policy. Where the Shipper or receiver requires help with loading or unloading, the Shipper provides it or pays for it (49 U.S.C. 14103). Lumper fees paid by the Carrier are reimbursed against a receipt when the Rate Agreement says so.

4.4 **Truck ordered, not used (TONU), layover and extra stops** are paid only as stated in the Rate Agreement.

## 5. Pickup, loading and delivery

5.1 The Carrier will arrive for appointments on time and follow each location's published rules, including safety equipment and check-in.

5.2 Where the Shipper loads and counts ("shipper load and count"), the Carrier is not responsible for concealed shortage or damage that cannot be seen at pickup, provided the seal is intact at delivery.

5.3 The Carrier will get a signed bill of lading at pickup and a signed proof of delivery at delivery, and will upload both to the portal.

## 6. Hazardous materials

6.1 When a load contains hazardous materials, the **Shipper** (as offeror) will:
- properly classify, describe, package, mark and label the materials;
- provide shipping papers (49 CFR Part 172, Subpart C) with a 24-hour emergency response telephone number (49 CFR 172.604).

6.2 The **Carrier** will:
- hold every registration, endorsement and permit required, including PHMSA registration where required (49 CFR 107.601);
- follow 49 CFR Parts 171–180.

6.3 Hazmat shipping papers are paper or regulatory documents under the hazmat rules. They are not replaced by an electronic signature in the portal (15 U.S.C. 7003(b)(3)).

## 7. Food (FDA Sanitary Transportation rule)

When a load is human or animal food covered by 21 CFR 1.900–1.934:
- (a) the **Shipper** gives the Carrier, **in writing**, its sanitary specifications for the vehicle and equipment, and the operating temperature, including any pre-cooling (21 CFR 1.908(b)). The Rate Agreement carries these specifications;
- (b) the **Carrier**:
  - supplies a vehicle that meets them;
  - pre-cools as specified;
  - can show the operating temperature on request (21 CFR 1.908(e)).

## 8. Safety and independence

8.1 Nothing in these Terms or in any Rate Agreement requires the Carrier or a driver to break any federal or state safety rule, hours-of-service limit or speed limit.

8.2 The Shipper will not coerce a driver to do so (49 CFR 390.6).

8.3 The Carrier is an independent contractor. It has sole control of its drivers, vehicles, routes and operations.

## 9. Invoicing and payment

9.1 The Carrier invoices **the Shipper** directly, with the documents listed in the Shipper's billing instructions.

9.2 The Shipper pays according to its stated payment terms, counted from receipt of a correct invoice with proof of delivery. Payment goes **directly to the Carrier**, or to the Carrier's factoring company when a notice of assignment is on file.

9.3 A change to payment instructions is valid only after the Shipper has confirmed it by phone with the Carrier on a number already on record.

9.4 No freight money passes through LoadBoot.

## 10. Loss and damage

10.1 The Carrier is liable for actual loss of or injury to the goods under 49 U.S.C. 14706 (Carmack). The only exception is where a **lower value** has been agreed in writing in the Rate Agreement, using the Shipper's declared-value election (49 U.S.C. 14706(c)(1)(A)).

10.2 Claims follow 49 CFR Part 370:
- the Shipper files a written claim that identifies the shipment and demands a specified amount;
- the Carrier acknowledges it within **30 days** and pays, declines or makes a firm offer within **120 days**.

The Shipper has at least **9 months** to file a claim and at least **2 years** from written disallowance to sue (49 U.S.C. 14706(e)).

10.3 **No waiver.** Neither party waives any right or remedy under 49 U.S.C. Subtitle IV, Part B, **except** where a Rate Agreement signed by both parties expressly says so (49 U.S.C. 14101(b)).

## 11. Insurance certificates

On request, the Carrier gives the Shipper a certificate of insurance showing the coverage in Section 2. The Carrier will not let that coverage lapse while carrying the Shipper's freight.

## 12. Indemnity

12.1 Each party indemnifies the other against third-party claims caused by its own negligence, its own breach of these Terms, or its breach of law.

12.2 The Shipper also indemnifies the Carrier against fines and losses caused by the Shipper's mis-declaration of weight, commodity or hazardous materials.

## 13. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's rates, customer names and facility information confidential. They are used only for the shipment.

## 14. Records

Both parties may keep the Rate Agreement, bill of lading, proof of delivery and tracking record stored in the LoadBoot portal. Electronic copies are originals for all purposes, except hazmat shipping papers (Section 6.3).

## 15. Term

These Terms apply to every load the parties agree through the portal. The Shipper may end them for future loads by closing its LoadBoot account. Loads already accepted are completed under these Terms.

## 16. Law and disputes

16.1 Federal law governs where it applies. Otherwise the law of **[STATE — owner/attorney to decide]** applies.

16.2 Disputes: **[COURTS / ARBITRATION — owner/attorney to decide]**.

## 17. Electronic signature

Each party agrees to sign electronically. A typed name, together with the recorded consent, time, IP address and text fingerprint, is a signature (15 U.S.C. 7001, 7006(5)).
