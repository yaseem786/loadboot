Google Play launch: app manifest, account deletion, listing assets

Groundwork for publishing LoadBoot on Google Play as a Trusted Web Activity,
plus the account-deletion feature Play requires of any app with sign-in.

--- One app, every portal ----------------------------------------------------

app/loadboot.webmanifest (new) - unified manifest scoped to /app/, so a single
installed app (and a single Play listing) covers carrier, partner and agent
rather than shipping three. start_url /app/, standalone, maskable icons, and
long-press shortcuts straight into each portal. The per-portal manifests stay
for anyone installing one portal on its own.

app/index.html - links the new manifest, and hides the Command Center and
Developers & API cards when launched as the installed app (CSS display-mode
plus an android-app:// referrer check, remembered for the session). Two reasons:
a carrier on their phone should never be offered a door marked "LoadBoot staff
only", and a store reviewer must not hit a portal they cannot sign in to -
that reads as "part of the app is inaccessible" and fails review. Both remain
fully available on the website.

--- Account deletion (migrations 0211, 0212) ---------------------------------

Play policy: users must be able to request deletion of their account and
personal data from inside the app, and the store listing must carry a public
page describing it. Neither existed.

Built as REQUEST + PROCESS, not an instant wipe, because an instant wipe would
be a promise we cannot legally keep: FMCSA requires 3 years of load and trip
documents, the IRS 7 years of invoices and settlements, and a carrier mid-load
cannot vanish while a broker still has a right to that trip record.

Backend:
  request_account_deletion / cancel_account_deletion / my_account_deletion_status
  cc_account_deletion_queue / cc_account_deletion_process
  Completing a request erases profile contact fields, the agent payout/tax/
  address block, org bank details, uploaded documents, chats and agent messages,
  and CRM/outreach traces, then closes the login permanently. The auth row is
  kept, not dropped, so retained financial records keep a stable foreign key.
  Requesting notifies staff in-app and sends the user a branded confirmation.
  anon-executable SECURITY DEFINER count unchanged at 27.

Frontend:
  app/carrier/app.js - "Delete account" card in Settings (serves both the
  carrier and agent portals). States plainly what is erased and what law
  requires us to keep, links the full policy, confirms before sending, and
  once open switches to a pending state with a Cancel button.
  app/shared/api.js - the five wrappers.

build_site.py - new public page delete-account.html, linked from the footer and
the legal navigation. Names LoadBoot LLC, gives both routes to request deletion
(in-app and privacy@loadboot.com), and itemises what is deleted, what is
retained and for how long, with the 2-day / 30-day / 90-day-backup timeline.
Build verified.

Still to do: the same Settings card in app/partner/app.js, whose settings view
has a different shape.

--- Play Console groundwork --------------------------------------------------

Four reviewer accounts created and sign-in verified (carrier, broker, shipper,
agent). Separate broker and shipper accounts are needed despite sharing one
dashboard, because their onboarding packets have no items in common - broker
wants FMCSA authority and a $75k BMC-84 bond, shipper wants a credit
application and payment terms.

Store listing prepared off-repo: title "LoadBoot Load Board & Dispatch" (30),
short and full descriptions, 1024x500 feature graphic, and eight phone
screenshots. The source screenshots were 1170x2532 - Play rejects anything
where the long side exceeds twice the short side (2.16), so each was padded
sideways onto the app background to land on 9:16 and converted from WebP,
which Play does not accept.

Migrations are applied to production; the JS and build changes need deploy.
