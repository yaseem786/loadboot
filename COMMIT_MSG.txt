Carrier truck intelligence: deep spec, VIN-to-COI matching, FMCSA cross-checks, and break-even

One day's work, all of it coming out of the same question: what did we have to ask a
carrier over WhatsApp that the product should already have known?

Add truck
- 30 new fields: truck domicile, door type and opening size, deck height, dock high,
  liftgate + capacity, pallet jack / ramp / e-track / load bars / straps / blankets,
  pallet positions, wheel-well width, temperature range, hazmat / TWIC / TSA / bonded /
  team, floor rate, max radius, home time.
- Rebuilt as a sectioned modal instead of one long column.
- Refuses a wheel-well gap wider than the cargo box.

VIN to certificate of insurance, live
- New cc_vin_coverage. On VIN blur the form says whether the truck is on the policy,
  before Save rather than after: green when covered, red with the VINs the policy does
  list, amber when no COI is on file.
- cc_pocket_upsert_truck now takes one jsonb payload. The old 14-arg signature stays as
  a thin wrapper so tabs open during the deploy keep working; the arg names do not
  collide, so PostgREST has no ambiguity.

Post a truck
- Pick a truck from the fleet and the posting fills itself in: domicile, equipment,
  floor rate, radius. Shows a "brokers will see" spec card and the certificate state,
  and refuses to post a truck the policy does not cover.

Loading profile — derived, not asked
- liftgate + dock height + pallet jack were being collected and never read, so a truck
  that cannot reach the ground could still be offered a residential drop. They now
  resolve to one capability: ground capable / dock or forklift / forklift or levelling
  dock / unknown. Deck height refines it — docks sit at 48-52in and most levelers reach
  about 12in below.
- cc_prebook_check gained a ground-delivery check. ADVISORY only: it is inferred from
  free text a broker typed, so a false positive must never block a real booking.

Know your number
- Carriers think in dollars per week; matching thinks in dollars per mile. New card
  turns one into the other: weekly cost in, break-even miles out, with a scenario table
  from 1,000 to 3,000 miles. The figure is volunteered, never required, and never
  leaves the carrier's own account.
- Round-trip preference, which had nowhere to live and is the thing carriers ask for
  most.

FMCSA cross-check
- Compares the fleet against the MCS-150: unit counts, hazmat claim vs the HM indicator,
  cargo classes, filing age, interstate vs intrastate, and whether the USDOT and MC on
  file actually belong to this carrier.
- This immediately found two live defects. Prime Freight's USDOT was stored a digit
  short and resolved to an unrelated inactive Canadian carrier; JMS's MC docket was a
  digit short. Both corrected.
- Census snapshots refresh nightly through pg_net.

Money and documents
- Bank account numbers and ABA routing numbers are validated, checksum included.
  Factoring remit-to now needs the factor's full account number, not the last four.
- Approved documents can finally be replaced. They had no Update button, so "every
  document is re-submittable" was only half true.
- Certificate holder is quoted as the finished four-line block everywhere. lc-doc-check
  had been asking carriers to "name LoadBoot" with no address at all.
- Onboarding collects the legal owner name, the W-9 line that had failed for every
  carrier who tried.

Fixes to things this work broke or exposed
- carrier_safety.authority_status did not allow 'unknown', which fmcsa-verify v26+
  always sends. Every FMCSA save had been failing silently since that deploy, because
  the edge function captures saveError and still returns ok:true.
- home_time had two vocabularies across two tables. They now share one, and the
  truck-level value wins over the carrier default.
- bl_sec_0225 closes an anon-grant path that revoke-from-public does not reach.

Migrations bl_fleet_0210 through bl_pref_0231, applied to staging and production.
esbuild clean. New file: app/carrier/economics.js.
