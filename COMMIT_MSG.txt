Google Play launch: unified app, account deletion, error monitoring

Three pieces of groundwork for publishing LoadBoot on Google Play as a Trusted
Web Activity, plus the observability that should have existed already.

--- One app, every portal ----------------------------------------------------

app/loadboot.webmanifest (new) - unified manifest scoped to /app/, so a single
installed app and a single Play listing cover carrier, partner and agent rather
than shipping three. start_url /app/, standalone, maskable icons, long-press
shortcuts into each portal. The per-portal manifests stay for anyone installing
one portal on its own.

app/index.html - links the new manifest, and hides the Command Center and
Developers & API cards when launched as the installed app (CSS display-mode plus
an android-app:// referrer check, remembered for the session). Two reasons: a
carrier on their phone should never be offered a door marked "LoadBoot staff
only", and a store reviewer must not hit a portal they cannot sign in to - that
reads as "part of the app is inaccessible" and fails review. Both stay fully
available on the website.

--- Account deletion (migrations 0211, 0212) ---------------------------------

Play requires users to be able to request deletion of their account and personal
data from inside the app, and the listing to carry a public page describing it.
Neither existed.

Built as REQUEST + PROCESS, not an instant wipe, because an instant wipe would
be a promise we cannot legally keep: FMCSA requires 3 years of load and trip
documents, the IRS 7 years of invoices and settlements, and a carrier mid-load
cannot vanish while a broker still has a right to that trip record.

  request_account_deletion / cancel_account_deletion / my_account_deletion_status
  cc_account_deletion_queue / cc_account_deletion_process

Completing a request erases profile contact fields, the agent payout/tax/address
block, org bank details, uploaded documents, chats and agent messages, and
CRM/outreach traces, then closes the login permanently. The auth row is kept,
not dropped, so retained financial records keep a stable foreign key. Requesting
notifies staff in-app and sends the user a branded confirmation.

  app/carrier/app.js - "Delete account" card in Settings (serves the carrier and
    agent portals). Says plainly what is erased and what law requires us to keep,
    links the policy, confirms before sending, then shows a pending state with a
    Cancel button.
  app/shared/api.js - the five wrappers.
  build_site.py - public page delete-account.html, linked from the footer and
    legal nav, with the 2-day / 30-day / 90-day-backup timeline. Build verified.

Still to do: the same card in app/partner/app.js, whose settings view differs.

--- Client error + performance monitoring (migration 0213) -------------------

Until now a broken deploy was invisible. The Command Center sat on its loading
screen for half an hour today and the cause - one missing export in api.js - had
to be found by reading Netlify and Postgres logs by hand. On phones that loop
does not exist at all: a carrier whose app breaks just leaves.

  app/shared/telemetry.js (new) - window.onerror, unhandled rejections, failed
    script/style loads, and Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS, FCP, TTFB) via
    PerformanceObserver with Google's thresholds. No third-party library, no SDK.
    Wired into the carrier, partner, command-center and developer entry points.
  app_private.client_errors / client_vitals, tele_scrub(), tele_ingest()
  public.track_web_event - EXTENDED, not replaced.

Three things make it worth having rather than noise:
  * Grouping. Errors are fingerprinted from a normalised message plus the top
    stack frame, so a loop throwing 5,000 times costs one row and one request.
    Each session sends at most 8 errors and never repeats one.
  * Privacy. Messages and stacks are scrubbed for emails, tokens and long digit
    runs on the device AND again in Postgres; query strings are dropped from
    routes. An error monitor that quietly accumulates user data is a liability.
  * Regressions. An error marked resolved that reappears on a newer build_id is
    reopened automatically - the silent regression is the case this is for.

anon-executable SECURITY DEFINER count unchanged at 27: telemetry rides the
ingestion door that was already anon-callable rather than opening a new one.

Still to do: the Command Center dashboard over these tables, and alerting on a
new error type or a spike.

--- Play Console groundwork --------------------------------------------------

Four reviewer accounts created and sign-in verified (carrier, broker, shipper,
agent). Broker and shipper need separate accounts despite sharing one dashboard,
because their onboarding packets have no items in common - broker wants FMCSA
authority and a $75k BMC-84 bond, shipper wants a credit application and payment
terms. Content rating came back Everyone / PEGI 3 with no descriptors.

Store listing prepared off-repo: title "LoadBoot Load Board & Dispatch" (30),
short and full descriptions, 1024x500 feature graphic, eight phone screenshots.
The source shots were 1170x2532 - Play rejects anything where the long side
exceeds twice the short side (2.16) - so each was padded sideways onto the app
background to land on 9:16 and converted from WebP, which Play does not accept.

Migrations are applied to production; the JS and build changes need deploy.
